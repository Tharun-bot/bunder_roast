A godown, a roaster & one stubborn idea

Kudla's Bunder was built on trade: coconut, tile, spice and fish, carried in and out for centuries. In 2019 we took over a former tile godown with its original laterite walls and put a 5 kg roaster where the sacks used to sit.

We still make the filter kaapi the way a Mangalore household does: decoction dripped overnight, hot milk, a pour from a height. Then we added V60s and said nothing. Rain-season mornings are our favourite: shutters half-down, buns warm, the harbour grey.
